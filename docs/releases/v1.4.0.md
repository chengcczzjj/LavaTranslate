### Changed

- **Android: no more system accessibility button.** Tap the LavaTranslate icon for the floating panel and × to close it; the Quick Settings tiles still work. Turning LavaTranslate on under Accessibility no longer adds the system's floating accessibility button at the screen edge, and its page now has a regular on/off switch.
- **Desktop: the original comes in from top to bottom.** Hold <kbd>Alt</kbd> (or use the eye button on the toolbar) to see the original: a thin line sweeps down the selection and each block of translation blurs out in turn to reveal the original. Let go and the translation comes back the same way. Pinned windows switch the same way.

### Upgrading on Android

- If you turned LavaTranslate on with 1.3.9 or earlier, the accessibility button stays on screen after the update, and tapping it now turns LavaTranslate's accessibility off. The first time you tap the icon after updating, the settings page shows how to remove it: under Accessibility, tap LavaTranslate 屏幕翻译 and turn off its Shortcut. Translation keeps working.

### 改进

- **安卓版：不再需要系统无障碍按钮**。点 LavaTranslate 图标弹出悬浮面板，点 × 关闭，下拉快捷开关照常可用。在系统「无障碍」里开启 LavaTranslate 时，不会再自动在屏幕边上加一个无障碍悬浮按钮，页面上就是普通的开关。
- **桌面版：看原文时从上往下切换**。按住 <kbd>Alt</kbd>（或点工具条上的眼睛）看原文：一道细光从选区顶部扫到底部，各段译文跟着依次模糊淡出、露出原文；松开时按同样的顺序切回译文。钉在桌面的窗口也一样。

### 升级说明

- 安卓版：用 1.3.9 及更早版本开启过的手机，更新后屏幕边上的无障碍按钮还在，而且现在点它会关掉 LavaTranslate 的无障碍。更新后第一次点图标会打开设置页，提示怎么移除：在系统「无障碍」里点 LavaTranslate 屏幕翻译，关掉「快捷方式」开关。翻译照常可用。
