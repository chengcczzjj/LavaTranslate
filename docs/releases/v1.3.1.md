### Fixed

- **Translated lines no longer overlap.** When a translation is longer than the original, the text now reflows within the paragraph like a browser would, keeping the original line-spacing ratio. If it still doesn't fit, it uses the empty space below the paragraph, but never goes past the next line of text. Only after that does the font get smaller. Previously, extra lines were stacked on top of the following text.
- **List markers stay put.** List markers such as ① • □ 1. that OCR picks up as separate pieces are left as they are, and text is no longer squeezed into them. This used to split words and leave a lone "s" in front of a line.
- Ordinary words are never broken in the middle. Only very long strings such as URLs or paths are split, and a short button label that really can't fit is hyphenated (e.g. "Recom-mended") instead of running into its neighbour.
- The taskbar no longer shows Electron's default icon.

---

- **译文不再叠在一起**：译文比原文长时，先在段落范围内像浏览器一样重新折行（行距按原来的比例）；还放不下再用段落下方的空白，但不会越过下一行文字；最后才缩小字号。以前多出来的行会直接压在下面的文字上。
- **列表符号保持原样**：被单独识别出来的 ① • □ 1. 等符号原样保留，不再把字硬塞进去（以前会把单词拆开，行首多出一个孤零零的 "s"）。
- 普通单词不再从中间拆开，只有网址、路径这类很长的字串才会断开；实在放不下的短按钮文字改用连字符分两行（如 "Recom-mended"），不再盖住旁边的文字。
- 修复任务栏显示 Electron 默认图标的问题。
