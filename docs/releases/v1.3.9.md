### Changed

- **Desktop: Space and Alt swapped.** Hold <kbd>Space</kbd> and the overlay steps aside so you can click and scroll the apps underneath (scroll a chat, press "Next"); let go to capture again. An existing selection stays and is re-translated if its content changed. Hold <kbd>Alt</kbd> to see the original and pause the translation; whatever is still coming in appears when you let go.
- **Desktop: double-click copies an image.** Double-click the selection to copy what you see (translated or original) as an image and close, like a screenshot tool. <kbd>Alt</kbd>+double-click copies the original image. <kbd>Enter</kbd> still copies all translations as text.
- **Android: system screenshots and screen recording work while translating.** The screenshot preview, screen-recording controls, notification banners, the volume panel and the accessibility button now appear above the translation instead of being hidden under it. Small ones show through the translation, which stays in place, so a screen recording captures the translation. When something larger opens (the full-screen screenshot preview, the notification shade, the power menu), the translation steps aside and comes back unchanged once it closes.
- **Android:** a system screenshot captures exactly what you see, translation included.

### Note

- Android: with the three-finger swipe, the translation steps aside as soon as your fingers touch the screen, so the screenshot may show the original. To capture the translation, press power + volume down. The capsule also appears in screenshots and recordings.

### 改进

- **桌面版：Space 和 Alt 对调**。按住 <kbd>Space</kbd>：遮罩暂时让开，可以直接点击、滚动下面的软件（翻聊天记录、点「下一页」），松开后重新截图；已有的选区会保留，画面变了自动重译同一块。按住 <kbd>Alt</kbd>：查看原文并暂停翻译，还没译完的松开后接着显示。
- **桌面版：双击复制图片**。双击选区，像截图一样把看到的画面（译文或原图）复制成图片并关闭；<kbd>Alt</kbd>+双击复制原图。<kbd>Enter</kbd> 仍是复制全部译文。
- **安卓版：翻译时可以正常系统截屏、录屏**。截屏预览、录屏控制条、通知横幅、音量条、无障碍按钮现在都显示在译文上面，不再被盖住。小的直接从译文里露出来，译文不动，录屏能录到译文；截屏后的整屏预览、下拉通知栏、关机菜单出现时，译文先让开，收起后原样放回来。
- **安卓版**：系统截屏截到的就是屏幕上看到的，包括译文。

### 说明

- 安卓版：三指下滑截屏时，手指一碰屏幕译文就让开了，可能截到原文；想截译文请用电源键 + 音量下。小胶囊也会出现在截图和录屏里。
