### Windows 1.4.6 更新

- **截图后直接滚动。** 按 **Win+空格** 打开截图，再按住空格滚动滚轮。也可以先松开 Win、继续按住空格。已有选区时，无需再点击选区；滚动期间鼠标下方的窗口会获得焦点，松开空格后恢复原选区。
- **识别和翻译分别显示。** 本地 OCR 时工具栏显示「识别中」。确认选区已是目标语言后，显示「已是目标语言」，跳过翻译请求。中文里夹有 OCR、API 或 Ctrl+C 等标记时，也会按中文判断。

### Windows 1.4.6

- Hold Space to scroll immediately after the capture shortcut or after drawing a region. The app under the pointer receives focus without an extra click. You can also release Win while continuing to hold Space.
- Local text recognition and translation now have separate status indicators. When the selection is already in the target language, the app skips the translation request. Chinese text containing technical labels such as OCR, API or Ctrl+C can also take this path.
