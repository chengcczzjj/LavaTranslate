### Changed

- **Reply and type-to-translate: pick any target language, or leave it on Auto.** The language menu now lists every language, including your own, with an **Auto** option at the top that tells you what it will pick. Your own language is now the interface language instead of the screenshot's target language, so switching screenshot translation to English no longer locks the reply box out of English.
  - Reply box: Auto translates into the language detected on screen. A language you pick holds until the next screenshot.
  - Type to translate (press the hotkey twice): no longer follows the screenshot settings. Auto translates both ways between your language and the foreign language you used last: type your language to get the other one, type anything else to get yours. A language you pick is remembered, and everything you type goes into it.
- **Desktop: pins land in the top-left corner.** A new pin flies from the selection to the top-left of the screen, and each further pin sits a little lower and to the right, so you can see it was pinned. It shows on top of your windows but no longer stays above everything: click another window and it can cover the pin. Turn on **Always on top** with the pin button in the pin's toolbar if you want it to stay up.
- **Desktop: pin the original.** Hold <kbd>Alt</kbd> and press <kbd>F3</kbd> (or click the pin button while holding <kbd>Alt</kbd>, or while the eye button shows the original) to pin the screenshot without the translation. Its copy button copies the original text.

### 改进

- **回复框和输入翻译：目标语言可以自动，也可以自己选**。语言菜单里列出全部语言（包括你自己的语言），最上面是「自动」，并写明此刻自动会译成什么。「你自己的语言」改为取界面语言，不再取截图翻译的目标语言——把截图翻译改成译英文后，回复框也能选回英文了。
  - 回复框：「自动」译成截图里识别出的对方语言；手动选的语言在这张截图里一直有效。
  - 输入翻译（连按两次快捷键）：不再跟着截图翻译的设置走。「自动」在你的语言和上次用的外语之间互译，输入中文译成外语，输入其他语言译成中文；手动选一种语言后，输入什么都译成它，下次打开还记得。
- **桌面版：钉图放到左上角**。钉好后从选区飞到这块屏幕的左上角，每钉一张往右下错开一点，一眼能看到已经钉好了。钉图显示在最上面，但不再一直压在所有窗口上：点了别的窗口就可以盖住它；需要一直在最上层时，点钉图工具条上的图钉。
- **桌面版：钉原图**。按住 <kbd>Alt</kbd> 再按 <kbd>F3</kbd>（或按住 <kbd>Alt</kbd>、开着「看原文」时点钉图按钮），钉的是不翻译的原图；复制按钮复制识别出的原文。
