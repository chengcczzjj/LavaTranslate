### New

- **Desktop: hold Alt to use the apps underneath.** In capture mode, hold <kbd>Alt</kbd> and the overlay steps aside: click, scroll and hover in the real apps (open a menu, scroll a chat, press "Next"). Let go and LavaTranslate captures again. If you had already selected a region, it stays selected; if its content changed, it is translated again automatically, otherwise the translation is kept as is.
- **Desktop: right-click exits** capture mode (it used to clear the selection). To select a different region, just drag a new one.
- **Android: long-press menu.** Long-pressing the idle floating button opens a menu with Exit, Settings, target language and quick reply, instead of jumping straight into quick reply. The menu during a translation uses the same order: close at the top, reply at the bottom.
- **Android: exit and power saving.** Exit (from the menu, the notification or Settings) stops the service and frees all of its memory. The button also exits on its own after an hour of disuse (adjustable) and when Battery Saver turns on, leaving a silent notification to bring it back. Idle and per-capture CPU use are lower.
- **New accent color:** the purple accents are now lava red, matching the icon, on both desktop and Android.

### 新功能

- **桌面版：按住 Alt 临时操作软件**。截图模式下按住 <kbd>Alt</kbd>，遮罩暂时让开，可以直接点击、滚动、悬停下面的软件（打开菜单、翻聊天记录、点「下一页」）；松开后自动重新截图。之前框选过的区域会保留：画面变了就自动重新翻译这一块，没变则译文原样保留。
- **桌面版：右键退出截图**（原来是取消选区）。想重新框选，直接拖一个新的即可。
- **安卓版：长按菜单**。悬浮球空闲时长按打开菜单（退出 / 设置 / 译成 / 快捷回复），不再直接进入快捷回复；翻译时的菜单也按同样顺序排列：关闭在最上，回复在最下。
- **安卓版：退出与省电**。从菜单、通知或设置里退出，会停止服务并释放全部内存；长时间不用（默认 1 小时，可调）或打开省电模式时自动退出，并留一条静默通知方便重新打开。待机和每次识别的耗电都更低。
- **新配色**：紫色点缀换成与图标一致的熔岩红，桌面版和安卓版都已更新。
