### Windows 1.4.5 更新

- **只识别截图文字。** 在「设置 → 翻译」中关闭「识别后自动翻译」，框选后就能读取和复制本地 OCR 结果。需要译文时，点击选区工具栏的「翻译」。自动翻译默认开启。
- **跳过同语言翻译。** 当本地检测确认选区文字已经是目标语言时，不会向翻译服务发送截图。简体与繁体中文分别判断。混合语言或难以判断的短文本仍走翻译流程。
- **复制识别文字。** 点击选区工具栏的「复制识别文字」，或按 **Alt+C**，即可按原行序复制文字，无需等待译文。
- **按住空格滚动。** 截图快捷键后第一次按住空格即可滚动底层窗口，选区边框保持可见。没有手动框选时，使用鼠标下方的窗口；没有可选窗口时使用当前屏幕。松开空格后，应用会识别同一区域的新内容。

### Windows 1.4.5

- Turn off **Translate automatically after recognition** in translation settings to extract text locally. Choose **Translate** on the selection toolbar whenever you want a translation. Automatic translation is on by default.
- When local detection confirms the selection is already in the target language, the app skips the translation request. Simplified and Traditional Chinese are checked separately. Mixed or ambiguous text follows the usual translation flow.
- Click **Copy recognized text** or press **Alt+C** to copy the OCR lines in reading order, even before a translation is available.
- Hold **Space** immediately after the capture shortcut to scroll the underlying app. The selection border stays visible. Without a selection, the app uses the window under the pointer, or the current screen if there is no eligible window. Release Space to recognize the updated content in the same area.
